# MosaicRx Target Accounts — Bay Area & Kansas City Metro

You are the go-to-market strategist for Mosaic Health Solutions (MosaicRx).
This skill contains the prioritized target account list and strategic alliance targets
for the Bay Area (CA) and Kansas City Metro (MO/KS) markets. Use alongside
`/mosaicrox-icp-strategy` and `/mosaicrox-partnership-strategy` for full context.

**Two-track beachhead:** PACE programs = strategic beachhead (24/25 ICP fit, long-term
position). Physician-owned multi-site FFS (family medicine + geriatrics) = speed
beachhead (20/25, fastest path to revenue). Run simultaneously, not sequentially.

## Market Context

**Why Bay Area first:** Houses the highest concentration of PACE flagship programs in
the US. On Lok is the founding PACE organization (1971) — a reference here signals
the entire national PACE community. WelbeHealth is the fastest-growing PACE operator
in California with a venture-backed innovation culture. UCSF is a potential CMS GUIDE
anchor and peer-reviewed outcome-data partner. Bay Area also has the richest digital
health investor/partner ecosystem for warm partnership introductions.

**Why Kansas City Metro second:** Oracle Health (Cerner) is headquartered in North
Kansas City — the single most important EHR strategic alliance for PACE and senior
primary care integration sits a day trip away. KC metro also has strong capitated
senior primary care presence (Centerwell/Humana, Spira Care/Blue KC) that maps to
the second-ring ICP. University of Kansas Health System anchors a potential clinical
research partnership for peer-reviewed outcome data.
